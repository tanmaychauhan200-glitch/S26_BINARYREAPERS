# HACKATHON DEMO WALKTHROUGH GUIDE

---

## 1. Setup & Launch
1. Ensure FastAPI backend is running on `http://127.0.0.1:8000`.
2. Ensure Frontend is running on `http://localhost:5173`.
3. Open browser in full screen.

---

## 2. 5-Minute Hackathon Demo Sequence

### Step 1: Mission Control Landing (30s)
- Showcase the Space-Grade Tactical Interface: 4 module cards with real-time health indicators and Indian regional focus.
- Highlight the core philosophy: *"One Planet. Four Perspectives. One Intelligence Platform."*

### Step 2: RiverWatch — Yamuna Delhi Sandbar Dynamics (75s)
- Select **RiverWatch** -> Choose **Yamuna River (Delhi Reach)**.
- Load bi-temporal comparison (Pre-monsoon 2023 vs. Pre-monsoon 2024).
- Use the **interactive split-screen swipe slider** to reveal active riverbank shift and exposed sediment area.
- Point out the cautious scientific terminology: *"Exposed sediment area increased by 14.2 ha — field investigation recommended."*

### Step 3: WetlandWatch — Deepor Beel Ramsar Site (60s)
- Switch to **WetlandWatch** -> Select **Deepor Beel (Assam)**.
- Display the **Water Persistence Frequency** map showing permanent core vs. seasonal water contraction.
- Highlight the 500m eco-sensitive buffer analysis.

### Step 4: ForestGuard & UrbanWatch Quick Highlights (60s)
- Showcase **ForestGuard** with Western Ghats canopy disturbance alerts.
- Showcase **UrbanWatch** with peri-urban Bengaluru built-up expansion encroaching on agricultural buffers.

### Step 5: Provenance & Export (45s)
- Click on **Data Provenance**: Display the Sentinel-2 L2A scene ID, orbit, SCL cloud mask percentage, and processing CRS (UTM 43N).
- Export analytical GeoJSON and CSV summary report.
